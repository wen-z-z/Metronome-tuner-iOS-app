# App: Metronome and tuner
This app serves as both a metronome and a tuner. There's a lot of visual clutter on the app and a lot of unused side features that contribute to the visual clutter, but the core functionalities are very useful, which makes it the perfect candidate to remake and strip down to its basic core functions.

## The problem
This app allows the user to use both a tuner and a metronome, which alleviates the issue of having to always carry them to practice or recitals.

## Features kept
The core features of having a 2 in 1 metronome and tuner will be kept. I also want to keep complex time signatures and accented beats. For the tuner, the microphone tuner as well as the note tuner will be kept. Since these features are fundamental to the product, they should be implemented into this app.

## Features added
* Instrument-specific tuners

With string instruments, the notes for each string are often not next to each other, and it makes the process of tuning more annoying than it needs to be. What instrument specific tuners hopes to achieve is to make the tuning process much simpler. Pick your instrument, and a UI replica of your instrument shows up and just click on the string to tune by ear. 
* Adjustable metronome sound

Sometimes a metronome sound is too sharp or jarring, or you’re trying to play off rhythms and you need the sound to be softer to focus. With adjustable metronome sound, you can either adjust the softness and clarity of the metronome sound, or upload your own sound.
